# Code of Conduct

Swell is a small project made by people in their free time. Everyone who
takes part in it, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else
the project is represented, is expected to help keep it a welcoming place.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing
  in a language that is not their first.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Keep criticism about code and ideas, never about people; explain the
  why.
- Accept that maintainers sometimes say no, and that their time is
  limited.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity,
  background or abilities.
- Sexualised language or imagery, threats, or encouraging others to do any
  of this.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after being asked to stop.
- Requests or content meant to help pirate music or get around TIDAL's
  subscription; Swell is for paying subscribers.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub (a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/oxakromax/swell-player/security/advisories/new)
works as a private channel too). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and
temporarily or permanently block people who break these rules, in
proportion to what happened.
